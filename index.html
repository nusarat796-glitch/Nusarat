🍡 Rava Ladoo

A delicious homemade Indian sweet.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rava (semolina)
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/4 cup ghee
  • Cardamom powder
  • Cashews and raisins

Preparation

  1. Roast the rava until aromatic.
  2. Let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix with powdered sugar and cardamom.
  4. Fry the nuts in ghee and add them.
  5. Add enough warm ghee to bind the mixture.
  6. Shape into ladoos while warm enough to handle.

🍮 Kesari Bath

A comforting, sweet semolina dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rava
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • Cardamom and cashews
  • A little food colour, if desired

Preparation

  1. Roast the rava in a little ghee.
  2. Heat the water separately.
  3. Carefully add the hot water to the rava.
  4. Stir until the rava cooks.
  5. Add sugar and stir until dissolved.
  6. Add ghee, cardamom and roasted cashews.
  7. Cook until the mixture comes together.
